Our Mission

Climb with purpose.
Return with perspective.

Alpine Ascents is a leader in mountaineering and climbing, offering expeditions that are challenging, safe and genuinely fun, from your first glacier walk to the roof of the world. We keep our teams small, pay our guides and porters fairly, and treat every mountain as a privilege.

  • Safety, always

    Certified guides, redundant communications and a turnaround-time culture. The summit is optional; coming home is mandatory.

  • Small teams

    Guide ratios as low as 1:2 on technical peaks, so every climber receives genuine coaching and attention.

  • Local partnership

    Every expedition is co-led with local mountain people, and a share of each booking supports community projects.

02 Why Alpine Ascents

Small teams,
experienced guides

Our IFMGA guides average fourteen seasons in the field. On most trips we run one guide for every three guests, and on the big objectives one to two — so every climber gets coaching rather than queueing.

  • Certified guides with a minimum of ten seasons, most with twenty or more.
  • Redundant safety systems: satellite messaging, radios, transceivers and a written emergency plan for every route.
  • Fair pay and progression for local high-altitude porters and Sherpa guides, with a share of each booking going to their communities.
  • A hard turnaround-time culture. The summit is optional; coming home is not.
