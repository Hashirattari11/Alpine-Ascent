02 Included as standard

What every booking covers

  • Guiding & safety

    IFMGA guides, a written route plan, emergency contacts shared with your family, and full rescue cover arranged through your insurer.

  • Technical kit

    Crampons, ice axe, harness, helmet, rope and glacier hardware supplied on technical trips, fitted to you in person before departure.

  • Mountain living

    Hut half-board or full expedition meals, tentage and group equipment, plus all permits and park fees.

  • Transfers

    Airport and trailhead transfers on arrival and departure days, including the Lukla flights for our Himalayan itineraries.

  • Small teams

    A maximum of six guests, and a guide ratio of 1:3 or better. Private departures can be arranged for couples and families.

  • Local partnership

    Local high-altitude porters and Sherpa guides paid at or above the standard rate, with a share of your booking supporting their communities.
