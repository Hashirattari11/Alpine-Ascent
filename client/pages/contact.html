Not at all — around half our guests arrive with no mountain experience. Our introductory Alpine course needs only walking fitness and a willingness to learn; your guide will fit crampons and teach every skill before it is needed on the mountain.

For trekking peaks, three or four hours of walking a day for a week. For technical 6,000ers you should be able to carry a 12 kg pack uphill for four hours. Our guidelines page has a twelve-week training plan to suit either.

Technical hardware — crampons, axe, harness, helmet, ropes and glacier kit — is provided on all technical trips and fitted for you in person. You will need your own boots, clothing layers, sleeping bag and personal items; we publish a full gear list per expedition.

Insurance covering mountaineering to your maximum altitude, including helicopter evacuation and repatriation, is mandatory on every trip. We check your policy before departure and will ask for the certificate; if you need a recommendation we can point you to specialists.

Every itinerary holds reserve days, and the summit is always optional. Your guide makes the call on the mountain, not the wish list: if the window closes we wait, we change the route, or we go home. Nobody has ever been left behind because of weather on our trips.
